Qi Xu 徐淇

I study how intelligent agents can form grounded representations of the physical world, reason about how it evolves, and use that understanding to act.

I am a PhD student at Westlake University, advised by Prof. Peidong Liu. My research connects 3D scene modeling, long-horizon video understanding, and multimodal post-training—building the perceptual and reasoning foundations for spatial world models that support embodied interaction.
